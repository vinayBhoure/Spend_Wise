## Key Limitations Summary

### Free Plan Restrictions:
- ✋ **3 accounts maximum**
- ✋ **6 months transaction history**
- ✋ **No custom categories** (system defaults only)
- ✋ **No budgets, goals, or subscriptions tracking**
- ✋ **No debts or investments tracking**
- ✋ **No advanced filters** (6M/Year/All Time periods)
- ✋ **No smart insights**
- ✋ **No CSV import or PDF export**
- ✋ **No cloud backup or multi-device sync**
- ✋ **Current month only** for most dashboard widgets

### Plus Plan Benefits:
- ✅ **Unlimited accounts**
- ✅ **Unlimited transaction history**
- ✅ **Full customization** (categories, labels, rules)
- ✅ **Complete financial toolkit** (budgets, goals, subscriptions, debts, investments)
- ✅ **Advanced analytics** (smart insights, long-term trends)
- ✅ **Professional exports** (CSV, PDF)
- ✅ **Cloud backup + multi-device sync**
- ✅ **Enhanced security** (biometric, PIN, encryption)

## Recommended For

### Free Plan:
- Students and young professionals
- Casual expense tracking
- Testing the app before committing
- Simple budgeting needs
- 1-2 bank accounts + cash

### Plus Plan:
- Serious budgeters
- Multi-account management (3+ accounts)
- Long-term financial planning
- Investment and debt tracking
- Need for historical analysis
- Multiple devices/backup required
- Power users who need exports and automation
